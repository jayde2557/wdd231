NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee for qualifying 501(c)(3) organizations
  • Listing in the online business directory
  • Access to monthly networking events
  • Discounted rates on chamber-hosted workshops

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Directory listing with business logo
  • Access to monthly networking events
  • One free event ticket per year
  • Newsletter mention twice per year

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Bronze, plus:
  • Priority placement in the directory
  • Quarterly spotlight eligibility on the home page
  • 20% discount on chamber event tickets
  • Access to member-only training sessions

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Silver, plus:
  • Guaranteed monthly spotlight rotation on the home page
  • Free booth at the annual chamber expo
  • Featured placement at the top of the directory
  • Complimentary tickets to all chamber events
  • Dedicated advertising space in the chamber newsletter
